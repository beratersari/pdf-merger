    How to arrange pages
    • Drag a page preview to reorder. Selected pages move together.
    • Delete takes a page out of the export. The original file is left as it is.
    • Rotate turns a page clockwise in the preview and in the export.
    • Export options can write a smaller file. Scanned pages shrink the most. Text in that copy is part of the picture.
    • Undo and Redo cover page edits. Ctrl+Z undoes. Ctrl+Y redoes.
    • Removing a file or clearing the workspace also clears undo.
